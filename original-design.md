## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an atmospheric, high-conviction digital environment tailored for modern local enterprises stepping into national prominence. Rooted in the visual concept of a *Digital Stage for Local Growth*, the aesthetic pairs deep cosmic exploration with polished editorial prestige. The mood is unapologetic, visionary, and authoritative—elevating grassroots commerce through the visual language of high-caliber technology and luxury achievement.

### Design Movement: Atmospheric Glass-Brilliance & Cosmic Elevation
The aesthetic synthesizes three key paradigms:
1. **Cosmic Immersion**: Midnight and abyssal navy planes layered to mimic infinite digital space, dotted with precise micro-constellations, orbital trajectories, and directional spotlight glows.
2. **Luminescent Glassmorphism**: Translucent floating containers with multi-stop border strokes mimicking rim lighting, combined with internal atmospheric diffusion (16px to 32px backdrop blurs).
3. **High-Value Metallics**: Prestigious gold light vectors (`#F6D36C`) that highlight achievements, tiers of business maturity, and critical points of interaction, creating high-contrast distinction against deep-ocean blacks.

### Personality & Tone
- **Prestigious yet Accessible**: Dignifying local MSMEs (Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises) with the sovereign elegance typically reserved for elite tech products.
- **Architectonic & Kinetic**: Structured layout grids paired with floating, zero-gravity dashboard surfaces and precision trajectory rings.
- **Audience**: Ambitious enterprise founders, digital-native artisans, venture scale adjudicators, and tech leaders seeking transformative economic tools.

## Colors

The color system operates on an asymmetric balance: **65% Dark Immersion (Midnight & Navies)**, **20% Structural Architecture (Panels & Cobalt)**, **10% Sovereign Gold (Frames, Highlights, Active Signals)**, and **5% Kinetic Accents (Coral & Lime)**.

### Surface Tiers & Deep Neutrals
- **Midnight (`#070A16`)**: Absolute ground canvas. Holds global ambient radial gradients and background orbital rings.
- **Deep Navy (`#0D1328`)**: Secondary zoning surface; used for major module sections, sidebar foundations, and sub-nav rails.
- **Panel Navy (`#151E3A`)**: Default standard elevated card surface; treated with 70–85% alpha when combined with glass backdrops.
- **Panel Bright (`#1D2A50`)**: Selected or hovered module background, input container surface, and interactive highlight zone.

### Sovereign Illuminations & Metals
- **Gold Primary (`#F6D36C`)**: The seed illumination. Serves as the primary signifier of excellence, active growth markers, primary metrics, and focal action framing.
- **Bright Gold (`#FFE99A`)**: Peak specularity, button inner-shadow glints, and radial micro-glow centers.
- **Gold Dark (`#A8792D`)**: Structural hairline borders, secondary active indicators, and subdued metadata divider rules.

### Action & Kinetic Accents
- **Cobalt (`#6078FF`)**: Core interactive engine; anchors navigation highlights, structural CTAs, and digital identity links.
- **Bright Cobalt (`#8096FF`)**: Dynamic hover fill, badge outer glow, and interactive active-state focus rings.
- **Coral (`#FF8270`)**: Urgent updates, high-velocity metric trends, and dynamic highlight chips.
- **Lime (`#B7E77A`)**: Positive economic trajectory, capital growth telemetry, verified status checkmarks, and operational health.

### Atmospheric Contrast & Borders
- **White Canvas Text (`#F8F3E6`)**: Off-white pearl for headline and primary content reading, tuned to prevent dark-mode glare.
- **Muted Text (`#AEB8D0`)**: Slate-cobalt neutral for secondary body copy, system descriptors, and inactive icons.
- **Glass Border (`#334264`)**: Hairline boundary stroke (typically at 40%–60% opacity) that separates depth planes without clutter.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the structured, sculptural rhythm of **Outfit** for headlines with the clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body and interface mechanics.

### Hierarchical Directives
- **High-Impact Display**: Headings leverage Outfit’s geometric construction at weights `700` and `800`. Letter-spacing is pulled tight (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) with leading clamped between `1.05` and `1.15` to project density and confidence.
- **Editorial Legibility**: Interface narratives and operational descriptions use Plus Jakarta Sans at `16px` and `18px`. Generous line heights (`1.6` to `1.7`) ensure reading ease across prolonged analytical sessions on high-density displays.
- **Micro-Telemetry & Badges**: Small metadata, status flags, and metric tags utilize `caption-caps` (`11px`, `800` weight, all-caps, with `+0.08em` tracking) to produce an authoritative aerospace/financial console aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an 8pt architectural rhythm structured around a responsive 12-column grid. Modules occupy clean proportion units that evoke floating command stages.

### Grid Anatomy & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px - 1440px+)**: 12 columns, max-width `1360px`, `32px` gutters, fluid margins minimum `48px`. Content-dense layouts deploy 3-column, 4-column, and asymmetric 8+4 module pairings.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, `24px` gutters, `32px` horizontal page margins. Multi-column cards collapse into paired dual-column groups.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns, `16px` gutters, `16px` outer gutters. Complex data displays convert into horizontally scrolling snap-strips or stacked cards.

### Layout Principles
- **Atmospheric Isolation**: Large vertical clearances (`space-3xl` and `space-4xl`) separate overarching thematic modules to keep midnight surfaces clear of visual crowding.
- **Dense Card Anatomy**: Internal card spacing stays compact (`space-md` to `space-xl`), creating sharp contrast between vast surrounding space and information-dense containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical and luminescent layering rather than flat opacity tiers. Surfaces float on defined z-planes above the midnight canvas.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Zero (Background)**: Solid Midnight (`#070A16`), marked by concentric CSS-rendered orbital track rings (`border: 1px solid rgba(51, 66, 100, 0.2)`) and deep radial cosmic plumes (`radial-gradient(circle at top, rgba(96, 120, 255, 0.08), transparent 70%)`).
2. **Base Level 1 (Sections & Shells)**: Deep Navy (`#0D1328`) with an ultra-fine border (`#334264`, 30% alpha).
3. **Elevated Level 2 (Floating Glass Panels)**: Surface color `rgba(21, 30, 58, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an ambient shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(0, 3, 15, 0.65), 0 0 0 1px rgba(51, 66, 100, 0.5);`
4. **Hero Level 3 (Active Cards & Modals)**: Surface color `rgba(29, 42, 80, 0.85)` framed with a multi-directional rim gradient stroke (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(246, 211, 108, 0.6) 0%, rgba(96, 120, 255, 0.2) 50%, rgba(51, 66, 100, 0.2) 100%)`). Shadows gain a tinted perimeter aura:
   `box-shadow: 0 24px 64px -16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 28px -4px rgba(246, 211, 108, 0.12);`

## Shapes

The interface balances precision geometry with soft, pill-shaped ergonomics (Roundedness category 3).

### Corner Radii Architecture
- **Interactive Badges & CTAs (`rounded-full`, 999px)**: Buttons, categorical indicator chips, search bars, and live performance metrics use true capsule forms.
- **Structural Modules & Surfaces (`rounded-2xl`, 28px)**: Master dashboards, showcase panels, and media containers use large, expansive rounded corners to maintain an organic, floating feel.
- **Nested Inner Modules (`rounded-xl`, 20px)**: Sub-panels, metrics tiles, and data list containers within primary modules maintain nested optical harmony.
- **Micro UI Form Factors (`rounded-md`, 12px)**: Input elements, tooltips, dropdown menus, and internal thumbnail viewports.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign**: Solid Gold (`#F6D36C`) background, text in Midnight (`#070A16`) with `weight: 800`. Radius `999px`. Inner rim glint (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 233, 154, 0.8)`). On hover: scale transform `1.02`, glow expands to `0 8px 24px rgba(246, 211, 108, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Cobalt**: Translucent Cobalt (`rgba(96, 120, 255, 0.15)`) fill, `#8096FF` text, border `1px solid rgba(128, 150, 255, 0.4)`. Radius `999px`. On hover: surface transitions to `rgba(96, 120, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Ghost Metallic**: Transparent fill, `#F8F3E6` typography, hairline border `rgba(51, 66, 100, 0.6)`. On hover: border brightens to Gold Dark (`#A8792D`) with white interior lighting.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges**: `rounded-full` enclosures with `caption-caps` typography (`11px/800`). Padding: `4px 12px`.
- **Category / Status Types**:
  - *Growth Positive*: Lime background tint (`rgba(183, 231, 122, 0.12)`), `#B7E77A` label, dot indicator with radial ping.
  - *Sovereign Scale*: Gold background tint (`rgba(246, 211, 108, 0.12)`), `#F6D36C` label, border `1px solid rgba(246, 211, 108, 0.3)`.
  - *Enterprise Core*: Cobalt tint (`rgba(96, 120, 255, 0.12)`), `#8096FF` label.

### Lists & Data Feeds
- **Card Rows**: List entries sit within Panel Navy containers separated by `8px` vertical gutters (avoid line-only dividers).
- **Interactive States**: Mouse-enter triggers a subtle `4px` horizontal translation and shifts the left rim border to illuminated Gold (`#F6D36C`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Control Shell**: 20px outer frame, `rounded-md` (checkbox) or `rounded-full` (radio). Inactive state: Deep Navy background with `1.5px` border in `#334264`.
- **Active State**: Primary Gold (`#F6D36C`) fill with Midnight check icon or radio pip, emitting a 4px ambient gold glow.

### Input Fields
- **Container**: Surface `Panel Navy`, height `52px`, `rounded-md` (12px) or `rounded-full` (for global search bars). Hairline border `1px solid #334264`.
- **Typography**: Input text in `#F8F3E6`, placeholder text in `#AEB8D0` at 60% opacity.
- **Focus State**: Surface elevates to `Panel Bright`, border transitions to `#6078FF`, backed by an outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(96, 120, 255, 0.25)`.

### Cards & Modules
- **Base Metric Card**: Panel Navy glass surface, `24px` padding, `20px` radius. Features a gold micro-bar on top or a trailing corner trajectory ring graphic.
- **Spotlight Showcase Card**: Uses an asymmetric gradient border containing gold-to-cobalt transitions. Contains an internal light gradient (`radial-gradient(circle at 80% 0%, rgba(246, 211, 108, 0.08), transparent 50%)`).

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Trajectory Orbital Gauge**: Circular progress visual that uses concentric hairpins to map business digitization tiers, with gold glowing heads indicating milestones.
- **Stage Presence Spotlight**: Header backdrop module with an angled, translucent cone of light (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(246, 211, 108, 0.07) 0%, transparent 80%)`) illuminating featured local enterprises.
